## About ImprintX

ImprintX builds tactile sensing systems for embodied intelligence and robotic manipulation. Starting from physical contact, we connect **devices, data, and models** to help robots perceive and understand the real world—and act on it with greater precision.

We work across the full tactile intelligence stack:

- **Tactile sensors** — high-resolution, low-latency perception of contact, force, deformation, slip, texture, and material properties.
- **Tactile data** — structured, multimodal physical interaction data for robot learning and evaluation.
- **Simulation and models** — scalable tactile simulation, perception, and control for contact-rich manipulation.
- **Robotic manipulation** — tactile feedback for dexterous grasping, precision assembly, and real-world deployment.

知行具身专注于具身智能与机器人精细操作。我们以触觉为入口，构建从器件、数据到模型的完整闭环，让物理接触成为可感知、可理解、可学习的数据。

## What we build

### Imprint X

An event-driven visuotactile sensor designed for fast, information-rich contact perception. It converts physical interaction into raw event data, 2D displacement fields, and 3D tactile deformation point clouds, and can be integrated into robotic fingertips and grippers.

[Explore Imprint X →](https://imprintx.cn/products)

### ImprintGlove

A high-dimensional tactile data acquisition system for embodied intelligence and human–robot interaction. It captures structured 3D contact-position and contact-force data from human manipulation to support multimodal robot learning.

[Explore ImprintGlove →](https://imprintx.cn/products)

## Open research & projects

We believe meaningful progress in robotic touch depends on tools that can be tested, reproduced, and improved by the community.

- **[TacFlex](https://github.com/Zhang-Chaofan/TacFlex)** — a multimodal visuotactile simulator for generating tactile images, marker motion, and 3D point clouds at scale.
- **GelStereo** — a family of stereo visuotactile sensors for high-resolution 3D contact geometry, spanning planar, palm-shaped, and biomimetic fingertip designs.

More code, datasets, models, and hardware resources will be published here as they become ready for the community.
